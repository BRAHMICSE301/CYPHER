PROTECT YOUR HARVEST

Six habits for safer grain

  1. Dry grain thoroughly before storage.
  2. Clean and inspect the storage space.
  3. Keep grain away from water and damp floors.
  4. Use suitable, clean storage containers.
  5. Check regularly for moisture, mould and insects.
  6. Ask your local agricultural expert for crop-specific advice.
