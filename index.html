📚 Grain Storage Best Practices & Standards

☀️

Safe Moisture Level

Always dry wheat to under 12% moisture and paddy/rice to under 13% before bagging. Grain with >14% moisture heats up and invites fungal Aspergillus growth.

🪵

Dunnage (Keep Off Floor)

Never stack bags directly on damp soil or concrete floor. Place wooden pallets or bamboo mats at least 15cm (6 inches) above the ground.

🛡️

Hermetic Packaging

Airtight multi-layer hermetic bags (PICS bags) suffocate insects naturally by depleting oxygen without needing harmful chemical fumigants.

🔍

Early Warning Clues

Check grain twice weekly. Clumping, warm grain pockets, or musty damp odors indicate moisture migration and immediate remediation needed.
