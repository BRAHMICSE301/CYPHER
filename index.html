📚 Grain Storage Best Practices

☀️

Safe Moisture Level

Dry grain adequately before storage. Suitable moisture limits vary by grain and storage duration.

🪵

Keep Bags Off the Floor

Use clean, dry pallets and leave space for ventilation. Prevent stored bags from contacting damp floors and walls.

🛡️

Protected Packaging

Appropriate sealed storage can reduce moisture exposure and insect damage when grain is sufficiently dry.

🔍

Early Warning Signs

Inspect for clumping, unusual warmth, visible mold, insects, or musty odors. Investigate problems promptly.
